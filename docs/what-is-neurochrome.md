# What is Neurochrome Vault?

Neurochrome Vault is a **desktop STL library manager** for Windows, built for people who download 3D printing models from Patreon, Kickstarter, and online marketplaces.

If you have been collecting models for more than a year, you already know the problem:

- hundreds of ZIP and RAR files scattered across folders
- the same model downloaded multiple times across different releases
- no way to search across your entire library
- you have to extract everything before you can preview anything

Neurochrome Vault replaces that workflow.

---

## The Silent Killer: Archive Extraction Chaos
Extracting **ZIP, RAR, or those cursed 7z archives** is not just slow—it's a crime against your creative time. Every time you extract a 5GB bundle just to find one specific head or base, you are wasting life energy and doubling your disk footprint. 

Stop being a file system janitor. Start being a creator. Neurochrome Vault gives you instant access without the extraction tax.

---

## What it actually does

- Organizes STL files by creator, release, and tags
- Lets you browse ZIP, RAR, and 7z archives **without extracting them**
- Previews 3D models directly — no slicer needed
- Searches across thousands of files in milliseconds
- Cuts disk usage in half through automatic deduplication

---

## Real numbers

On a real wargaming vault with 362 releases from 85 creators:

- **396 GB** of STL files stored in **197 GB** on disk
- **199 GB saved — 50% smaller**
- Zero duplicates. Zero orphaned data.

---

## Who it's for

- Resin and FDM hobbyists with large Patreon backlogs
- Wargamers collecting miniature STL releases
- Anyone whose STL library has grown beyond what folders can manage

---

## Why not just use folders?

Folders break down when:

- your library exceeds 100 GB
- the same model appears in multiple bundles or release versions
- you need to find something without remembering exactly where you put it

ZIP and RAR don't solve this either — they compress files but give you no way to search, deduplicate, or browse without extracting.

---

## The key idea

Neurochrome Vault treats your entire collection as a **single system**, not a pile of isolated archives. Every release, every creator, every file — indexed, deduplicated, and instantly browsable.
