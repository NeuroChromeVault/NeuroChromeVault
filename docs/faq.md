# FAQ — Neurochrome Vault

## Do I have to import everything into the vault?

No. Neurochrome Vault can browse and preview ZIP, RAR, and 7z archives directly without importing anything. The vault and its deduplication are optional — you activate them when you want the storage savings.

---

## How much space can I save?

On a real 362-release wargaming vault: **199 GB saved on a 396 GB library — 50% reduction**.

Results vary depending on how many releases you have from the same creators. The more version variants and monthly drops from the same creators, the higher the savings. One creator in the test vault showed 74% reduction across 5 releases.

---

## Does it modify my STL files?

No. Your geometry is stored bit-for-bit identical to the original. Any file can be exported back to a standard STL at any time.

---

## Does it work on external drives or NAS?

Yes. The vault can live on any drive — internal SSD, external USB, or network storage. Performance tested on a USB 3.0 drive at ~38 MB/s.

---

## How fast does the viewer open models?

Average first-frame time on a 116 MB STL stored in a compressed archive on a USB drive: **782 ms cold, 114 ms cached**. Under a second to see the model start appearing, with no extraction step.

---

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything runs locally. No account, no cloud, no internet required.

---

## Can I search across my entire library?

Yes. NCV indexes all content — by creator, release, file name, and tags. Search works across your entire vault in milliseconds.

---

## Is it a subscription?

No. Neurochrome Vault is a one-time purchase.

---

## What operating system does it support?

Windows. Other platforms are not currently supported.

---

## Who is it for?

Anyone with a growing STL library who downloads from Patreon, Kickstarter, or marketplaces — resin and FDM hobbyists, wargamers, and miniature collectors.
